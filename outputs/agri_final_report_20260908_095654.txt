**B. Immediate recommendation**  

Because the field is water‑logged during the monsoon and the soil is a deep‑black, moisture‑retentive black cotton soil, the safest high‑profit option for the coming Kharif season is **Kharif sorghum (jowar) or pearl millet (bajra)**. Both are:

* tolerant to temporary water‑logging,  
* well‑suited to black cotton soils,  
* require moderate N (which you can supply), and  
* give good market returns in Maharashtra.

If you can invest in proper field drainage (bunds, shallow ditches) you may also consider **cotton** as a higher‑value option, but it is riskier until drainage is reliably improved.

**D. Why the selected crops were chosen**  

1. **Sorghum & Pearl millet** – Both crops are explicitly mentioned in the advisory to *“Remove the excess amount of water stagnated … in the Kharif sorghum / pearl millet crop”*【Evidence 4】, indicating that they are grown in this region and can tolerate occasional water‑logging if drainage is provided. Black cotton soils are ideal for these cereals, and the high K level (100 kg ha⁻¹) matches their nutrient needs.  

2. **Cotton** – Gives higher returns, and black soils are traditionally good for cotton, but the same advisory warns that *“water logging … should be avoided”*【Evidence 2】. Hence it is placed lower, pending drainage improvement.  

3. **Pulses (mung bean, black gram)** – While they can be grown in rainy season, the advisory notes they are *“susceptible to water logging”*【Evidence 5】, making them risky on the present field.  

4. **Rice** – Needs controlled standing water; with already poor drainage it would increase water‑logging and disease pressure, so it is not recommended for profit under current conditions.

**G. Soil and irrigation guidance**  

- **Soil health:** Black cotton soils swell when wet; avoid deep tillage after heavy rain to prevent hard‑pan formation.  
- **Drainage:** Permanent shallow ditches or raised beds are essential; keep them clear of debris.  
- **Irrigation:** For sorghum/millet, **no supplemental irrigation** is needed unless a dry spell > 10 days occurs. If you irrigate, do it **early morning** to allow quick drying and avoid fungal build‑up.  
- **Fertiliser timing:**  
  * Basal N‑P‑K at sowing (see above).  
  * **Second N split** at 30 days after sowing (DAS) – 30 kg ha⁻¹ (≈ 7.5 kg ac⁻¹).  
  * **Third N split** at 60 days DAS – same amount. Adjust based on leaf colour and soil test.
